Headline numbers. Of the 442 edge probes: 98 identical in every table, 129 accepted by both
with a table difference (most of those are one systematic cosmetic difference, E1), 92 refused by
ASM6x and accepted by asm6x, 37 the other way, 86 refused by both. The two fresh compiler files
(a 140 KB frame, a 26-case switch, every string escape, floats, doubles, 64-bit arithmetic;
C++ with throw/catch, a virtual hierarchy, a static with a destructor) assembled identically
and linked. All 763 corpus files assembled on 763 threads in one invocation under
ThreadSanitizer: no report, and every object byte-identical to the one-at-a-time object; the
release build does the same in 0.5 s. The fixed bytes for `.c6xabi.attributes`,
`.TI.symbol.alias` and `.TI.section.flags` were checked against asm6x's objects for files of
2 to 45 sections: constant, as ASM6x assumes. No object ASM6x wrote was rejected by lnk6x, so
the "lnk6x-rejects" class is empty; where the assembled tables differ, the linked programs
differ (A2, A3, A5, A6, A7, A1) or do not (A8, E1, E2, E4), and each entry says which.

Severity classes and ids: **A** wrong-object (silent), **B** refusal-of-legal-input,
**C** crash-or-hang, **D** out-of-scope-gap, **E** cosmetic (table differs, lnk6x indifferent).
Ranked within each class; A first.

## A. Silent wrong objects

**A1. `.usect` is wrong in every field.** `x .usect ".far", 8, 4` gives `.far` size 4, align 1,
and `x` a FUNC symbol at offset 0 of `.text`; asm6x: `.far` size 8 align 4, `x` an OBJECT at
`.far`+0 with st_size 8 (asm6x records the size of a `.usect` symbol even when local, unlike a
local `.bss` symbol's). Three causes: `statement()` (src/c6x.cpp:89-92) defines the column-1
label in the *current* section before the directive runs; `directive()` (src/c6x.cpp:168-172)
takes the size from `cuts[k + 1]` with `k = 1`, which is the alignment's slot, and the alignment
from a slot that does not exist; nothing sets `size`. `y .usect ".mybss", 3` (no alignment) is
refused outright. Linked: `linkcheck/usect` loads a different address (mine's MVKL/MVKH pair
resolves to `main`'s address). Probes: edge 200-203, linkcheck/usect. Same root for
`lab: .bss b, 4, 4`, whose `lab` asm6x puts in `.bss` (edge 213). Fix: hand the pending label to
the directive instead of defining it first; for `.usect` read name/size/align from cuts[0..2];
define the symbol in the new section as OBJECT with `size`; refuse a `.usect` without a label
(asm6x: "Symbol expected in label field").

**A2. A branch or call addend to a symbol outside the section is dropped.** `B ext+8`,
`CALLP ext+4, A3`, `B far-4` (far in `.text:far`): asm6x writes the addend in the REL field
(`00000112` = +2 words; `10000090`), ASM6x writes `00000012`/`10000010`. `Unit::resolve()`
(src/unit.cpp:262-268) writes the in-place addend only for ABS32/ABS16/ABS8. lnk6x uses the
field: `linkcheck/addends` links to a program whose two branches land 2 words short
(`0000b592` vs `0000b692`, `0000b192` vs `0000b112`). The same omission hits PREL31/EHTYPE:
`.word $EXIDX_FUNC(f)+4`, `$EXTAB_LP(lp)-4`, `$EXTAB_RTTI(t)+4` (edge 419, 420, 425; asm6x
`04 00 00 00`/`fc ff ff ff`, mine zeros) - there lnk6x happened to produce the same program
(`linkcheck/exidx-addend`), so that half is a table difference only. Fix: in resolve(), for
PCR_S21 put `addend/4` into bits 7-27, for PCR_S12 into bits 16-27 (see A3), for PREL31/EHTYPE
the 32-bit addend in place; refuse an addend that is not a multiple of 4 or does not fit.

**A3. BNOP's displacement is 12 bits, and its relocation is R_C6000_PCR_S12, not PCR_S10.**
C64x+ BNOP (displacement form) carries scst12 in bits 16-27. ASM6x uses `R_PCR_S10` (6) for a
target outside the section where asm6x writes `PCR_S12` (5) (edge 096), and lnk6x then applies
a 10-bit relocation at bits 13-22, clobbering the count field: `linkcheck/bnop-external` links
`0003a120`/`007d4120` where asm6x's object gives `001a6120`/`0fe84120`. In-section targets
512..2047 words away are refused ("too far", edge 114) where asm6x encodes them
(`02000120`). src/c6x.cpp:472, src/unit.cpp:246-249 (`bits = 10`). Fix: `fix_kind = R_PCR_S12`,
`bits = 12, shift = 16` for it, and the in-place addend (A2). (PCR_S10 belongs to BDEC/BPOS,
which the compilers do not write.)

**A4. A negative parenthesised offset is silently re-read as a bracketed one.**
`LDW *+B15(-4), A2` encodes as `*+B15[2]` (`013c42e4`) because `mem_operand()` marks the `[n]`
form by storing `-2 - n` (src/c6x.cpp:283) and the load decodes any `v <= -2` as units
(src/c6x.cpp:641); asm6x writes `*-B15[1]` (`013c20e4`, edge 134). `*+B15(-8)` becomes `[6]`.
Fix: carry a `bracket` flag in `Operand`; a negative offset flips `minus` as asm6x does, or is
refused.

**A5. The unit guess that guards `||` is wrong for six families, so a packet can be accepted
with two instructions on one unit, and legal packets are refused.** `classifier-check.py` over
the 4547 unit-bearing words of the recorded encodings: 51 guessed wrong - every C64x+ `.D`
form (AND/OR/XOR/ANDN, the crossed ADD/SUB: guessed S), MPY32U (guessed S) and MPY32US
(guessed D), ADDSP/SUBSP/ADDDP/SUBDP (guessed L; asm6x's default is the `.S` form), and the
`.S` SUB/SUBSP with a crossed first source (guessed D). src/c6x.cpp:677-684 derives the unit
from the low form bits, and those families break the pattern. Consequences, all confirmed:
`ADD A1,A2,A3 || AND A4,A5,A6` and `SUB B1,A2,A3 || SHL A4,A5,A6` are accepted with both
instructions on `.D1` / both on `.S1` (`031489b0`, `01845d71`); asm6x moves the second to
`.S1`/`.L1` (`031487e0`, `018832f9`), and `linkcheck/packets` links ASM6x's version into a
program the core would fault on (edge 155, 157). Refused though legal for asm6x: `AND || SHL`,
`AND || OR`, `MPY32US || LDW`, `MPY32U || SHL`, `MV || MV`, `MV || LDW`, `NOT || LDW`,
`MVK || CMPEQ`, `SUB B1,A2,A3 || ADD` (edge 154, 156, 158, 160-166). The compilers never write
`||`, so nothing shipped is affected. Fix: give each `Form` its unit letter in the tables (the
oracle's listing names it) instead of deriving it; then the refusal is at least honest. (Moving
an instruction to a free unit as asm6x does stays out of scope by the README.)

**A6. An instruction after data in a code section is emitted unaligned.** `.byte 1` then
`MVK 7, A1`: ASM6x emits the word at offset 9 (`9ca35801` straddling two words), asm6x pads to
0xc; a label on its own line before the instruction takes the padded address in asm6x (edge 336:
`fwd` at 0xc vs 0x9). `linkcheck/text-align` links two different programs. Fix: `u.align(4)` at
the top of `instruction()`; define a label that stands alone on its line lazily, at the next
emission, or align it when the section is code.

**A7. `MPY32 A1, A2, A5:A4` encodes the 32-bit MPY32.** A pair destination falls through to
`kThreeReg`'s `{"MPY32", 0x200}` and writes dst = A4 (`02082800`); asm6x writes the 64-bit
result form (`02082a00`, form 0x280) (edge 077, linkcheck/mpy32-pair). The emulator's checker
takes a pair destination on any three-operand integer form, so this is inside the shared
vocabulary. Fix: a pair-destination entry for MPY32 (0x280), and refuse a pair destination on
the other kThreeReg entries that have none.

**A8. `.sect` flags follow a name list; asm6x's follow the `RW` keyword and the content.**
asm6x: a `.sect` is PROGBITS with SHF_ALLOC only (0x2) unless `, RW` is written (then 0x3);
it becomes executable (0x6, align 32) when an instruction lands in it, whatever its name;
`.data` is 0x3 and only `.bss`/`.usect` make NOBITS. ASM6x (src/unit.cpp:61-88): writable
unless the name head is in a read-only list, code if the head is `.text`, NOBITS if the head is
`.bss` or `.far`. Differences seen: `.sect ".foo"`, `.cinit`, `.fardata`, `.neardata` without
RW, `.data:x`, `.fini_array`, `.stack`, `.sysmem`, `.cio`, `.vm6747.eh` get 0x3 for asm6x's 0x2
(edge 338-368, 372); `.sect ".far"` / `.sect ".bss"` / `.sect ".bss"`+label become NOBITS and
the `.word` written into them is **discarded** (edge 214, 215, 409: asm6x has `01 00 00 00`,
ours nothing); a `.text:f` holding only data is 0x6/align 32/padded to 32 for asm6x's
0x2/align 4 (edge 351), an empty `.text:f` 0x6/32 for 0x2/1 (350); `.sect ".textfoo"` and
`.sect ".TEXT"` with code are refused ("outside a code section", edge 352, 371) where asm6x
assembles them as code. lnk6x placed both variants identically in the probes
(`linkcheck/sect-flags`, `sect-far-word` link to the same program, the `.far` data aside), and
the compilers only write names the list gets right, so today this is a table difference plus
the data loss in `.sect ".far"`. Fix: `.sect` -> PROGBITS, A, +W on `RW`, +X (and align 32) on
the first instruction; NOBITS only from `.bss`/`.usect`; `.data` -> A+W.

**A9. String escapes: `.string` is raw in asm6x, `.cstring` takes the full C set; ASM6x
applies a partial set to both.** asm6x's `.string "a\nb"` is the four bytes `a \ n b`,
`"\\"` two bytes; its `.cstring` turns `\101` into `A`, `\x41` into `A`, `\a\b\f\v` into
`07 08 0c 0b`. ASM6x's lexer (src/lexer.cpp:117-130) handles `\n \t \r \0 \\ \"` for every
string and passes anything else through as the letter, so `.string "a\nb"` is 3 bytes,
`.cstring "\101"` is `1 0 1 NUL`, `"\a\b\f\v"` is `a b f v`, `"\xff\377"` is `x f f 3 7 7`
(edge 217-230, 242). Also `.cstring "z\0"`: asm6x 2 bytes, ours 3. No compiler writes
`.string`/`.cstring` (the census: none in 763 files), so nothing shipped is affected. Fix:
no escape processing in the lexer; `.cstring` decodes C escapes (`\a \b \f \n \r \t \v \\ \" \'
\xHH \ooo`) itself and does not add a second NUL after an explicit `\0`; `.string` stays raw.

**A10. Range checks that let a wrong object out.** `.bss b, 4, 0x100000000` -> sh_addralign 0
after the `(int)` cast (UBSan: division by zero at src/unit.cpp:175); `.bss b, 0x100000000, 4`
-> 4 GB pushed, then st_size/sh_size truncated to 0; `.align 0x40000000` -> a 1 GB section
(asm6x: "Maximum alignment is to 32K boundary - alignment ignored"); `.space -1` -> nothing
(asm6x writes 512 MB, no better, but silent nothing is worse); `.word 0x100000000` /
`4294967296` truncated without a word (asm6x warns); `CALLP ext, B4` encoded as if B3
(`10000012`; asm6x: "A3 or B3 required as return address"); `[A0] CALLP ext, A3` ORs the
predicate into CALLP's fixed high bits (`d0000010`; asm6x: "must be unconditional"). Probes:
crashbox/*, crash/*, edge 101, 102, 252-255. Fix: cap `.align` and `.bss`/`.usect` alignment at
32768, sizes and `.space` at 2^31-1 and >= 0, refuse a `.word`/`.half`/`.byte` outside its
range as it already does for the two small ones; CALLP's register must be A3/B3 and takes no
predicate.

**A11. A label difference across sections in an instruction is silently cut to its first
label.** `MVKL fwd-d, A0` and `B fwd-d` with `d` in `.data`: ASM6x relocates against `fwd`
alone, the `-d` vanishing (src/c6x.cpp:462-496 read `val.sym` and `val.v`, never `val.sub`);
asm6x refuses ("Unable to resolve this relocatable expression") (edge 432, 433). Fix: refuse an
operand whose `val.sub >= 0` after evaluation.

**A12. An exidx table opened before its code section links to `.text`.** `Unit::section()`
finds the section an `.c6xabi.exidx:<code>` describes only among sections already opened
(src/unit.cpp:80-84), so `.sect ".c6xabi.exidx:.text:f"` written before `.sect ".text:f"`
gets sh_link 1 (`.text`) where asm6x writes 2 (`.text:f`) (edge 360; c6xdiff does not compare
sh_link, elfdump shows it). cpp11 writes the exidx after the code, so not hit. Fix: resolve
`linked` by name in write_elf().

## B. Refusals of legal input (asm6x accepts and encodes)

**B1. Constant forms of AND/OR/XOR/MPY/LMBD and the unsigned compares.** `AND A1, 5, A2` (asm6x
turns it about: `AND.D1 5,A1,A2`, `0104a9f0`; likewise OR, XOR), `MPY 5, A1, A2` and
`MPY A1, 5, A2` (`MPY.M1 5,A1,A2`, `0104ac00`; `MPY -16` too, not MPYU/MPY32), `LMBD 1, A1, A2`
(`01042d58`), `CMPGTU/CMPLTU` with 16..31 (asm6x encodes 31; ASM6x stops at 15 - the emulator
allows 0..31) and `CMPGTU A1, 20, A2` (edge 018-020, 025-027, 030-033, 035). All are inside
the emulator's checker ("either source may be a constant"). Fix: entries in kCstFirst for
AND/OR/XOR are there already - route the register-first spelling to them; add MPY (`.M` scst5,
form 0x300 per `0104ac00`) and LMBD (0x356 per `01042d58`); ucst5 for the unsigned compares.

**B2. A constant with a crossed source, and asm6x's negative-constant rewrites.**
`ADD 3, B1, A2` / `ADD B1, 3, A2` / `ADD B1, -4, A2` -> `ADD.D1X 3,B1,A2` (`01047af0`);
`SUB B1, 3, A2`; `SHL/SHR/SHRU B1, 3, A2` -> `.S1X` (`01047ca0`); `ADD -3, A1, A2` ->
`SUB.D1 A1,3,A2`; `ADD -17, A1, A2` and `ADD A1, -17, A2` -> `SUB.D1 A1,0x11,A2` (ASM6x's
`ADD` with a negative constant stops at -16). Edge 044, 047, 048, 051, 054-059. Fix: allow x on
the `.D` scst5 form and the `.S` shift forms; turn `ADD r, -c` for c > 16 into `SUB r, c` up to
31 as asm6x does.

**B3. The 15-bit address adds from B14/B15.** `ADDAW B15, 100, B3` (`118064fe`),
`ADDAB B14, 1000, B3`, `ADDAH B15, 32767, B3` (`11ffffde`), `ADDAW B15, 32768, B3` (asm6x
accepts even that, wrapping) - the C64x+ `ADDA* .D2 B14/B15, ucst15, dst` forms, which the
emulator's checker allows up to 32767 (edge 066-069). Fix: the ucst15 form (the constant in bits 8-22, y in
bit 7, the three op bits in 4-6 and `11` in 2-3 - read it back from the four words above) when
the base is B14/B15 and the constant exceeds 31.

**B4. The 40-bit forms with a pair source, and NORM of a pair.** `ADDU A1:A0, A2, A5:A4`
(`02004538`), `ADD A1:A0, A2, A5:A4` (`02004438`), `ADD B1, A2, A5:A4` / `SUB B1, A2, A5:A4`
(`.L1X`, `02045478`), `NORM A5:A4, A2` (`01100c18`) (edge 072-075, 084). In the emulator's
vocabulary (ADDU/SUBU take a pair source; NORM is a unary). Fix: the `.L` pair-source forms.

**B5. `MVK sym, A0`.** asm6x writes `MVK.S1 0,A0` with a RELA `R_C6000_ABS_S16` (kind 8, which
`RelKind` already names) (edge 005, 006). Fix: `fix_kind = R_ABS_S16`, written as RELA.

**B6. BNOP without a count, and counts 6 and 7.** `BNOP fwd` is count 0 for asm6x; `BNOP fwd, 6`
is accepted (`0001c120`, the field is 3 bits). ASM6x demands a count and stops at 5 (edge 098,
099). Fix: default 0; accept 0..7 (the emulator also stops at 5; align the two).

**B7. Syntax asm6x takes that ASM6x refuses.** `.align` alone (asm6x: no-op, align 1; edge 315,
316); `al .set fwd` / `al .set ext` aliases (asm6x resolves `B al` in place and writes `al` as
an absolute of value 4; edge 108, 298-300); `.symdepend "sym"` with one operand (the current
section), an unquoted symbol, or a section opened later (edge 308, 309, 311); `.asg` (ignored,
then the name is undefined; 307); `.float 1.5` / `.double` (262, 263); `.sect .foo` unquoted
(370); `<<`, `>>`, `&`, `|`, `^`, `%`, `~` in expressions (266-269, 291); `1010b`, `17q`
(246, 247, 250); `''''` (245); `.word "a"` / `.half "ab"` (a one-character string as a value;
236, 237); lowercase `a0`, `[a0]` (asm6x accepts `mv a0, b1`; 115, 116); code in a section
not named `.text…` (352, 371, see A8). None of these appear in the compilers' emissions.

## C. Crash or hang

**C1. Operand-less directives read past the token vector.** `.global`, `.ref`, `.weak`,
`.sect`, `.word`, `.byte`, `.string`, `.cstring` with no operand, and `.byte 1,` (a trailing
comma): `split_items()` always yields n >= 1 with `cuts[0] == t.size()`, and `directive()`
indexes `t[cuts[0]]` (src/c6x.cpp:159, 189, 214, 224). ASan: heap-buffer-overflow /
container-overflow (`crash/*.asan.log`, edge 288, 369, 392-398). The release build "accepts"
`.global` alone and writes a global symbol with an empty name (edge 392). Fix: `n = 0` when
`cuts[0] >= t.size()`, and each directive's `n < needed` check before any `t[cuts[k]]`.

**C2. Huge alignments and sizes hang or exhaust memory.** `.align 0x80000000` and
`.align 0x100000000` never return (the `(int)` cast gives a negative or zero modulus at
src/unit.cpp:175; UBSan reports the division by zero); `.align 0x40000000`, `.space
0x100000000`, `.bss b, 0x7fffffffffff, 4`, `B fwd` over a `.space 0x100000000` push bytes for
as long as memory lasts (30 s alarm in `crashbox/ASM6X-RESULTS.txt`, `crash/`). asm6x: warns
and ignores an alignment over 32K, truncates a constant to 32 bits with a warning. Fix as A10.

**C3. Number lexing overflows `long long`.** `.word 99999999999999999999` and
`0x123456789ABCDEF0123`: signed-overflow UB at src/lexer.cpp:38 and :68 (UBSan; edge 264,
265). asm6x warns "truncated to 32 bits". Fix: accumulate unsigned and refuse past 64 bits.

## D. Out-of-scope gaps (brief, by the README's own list)

D1 units named in the source (`ADD .L1 A1,A2,A3`: asm6x `01882078`; refused here by design).
D2 moving a colliding instruction to a free unit (see A5 for the honest-refusal precondition).
D3 register-offset addressing `LDW *+A4[A5], A6` (asm6x `0310aa64`; refused: "'A5' is not
defined"). D4 `$` (asm6x: a relocation against the section symbol). D5 `.float`/`.double`,
`.asg`, `.field`, macros, the operators of B7. D6 compact instructions, DWARF, ADDKPC - not
probed. D7 ASM6x's own extensions asm6x lacks, all benign: forward `.set` constants in `.bss`,
`.align`, `.space`, `*+B15(K)`; forward label differences in `.word`/`.half`/`MVKL`; the
`.set name, value` spelling (asm6x: "Label required").

## E. Cosmetic - the tables differ, lnk6x does not care

**E1. An empty `.text` is 32-aligned; asm6x's is 1.** Every data-only file differs in c6xdiff
by this one line (edge 186, 194, 195, 198, 206-209, and ~60 more); `linkcheck/text-align-empty`
links identically. Fix: raise a code section's alignment to 32 when the first instruction is
emitted (asm6x also gives a data-only `.text:f` align 4 - A8).

**E2. MVKL/MVKH with an addend: asm6x also writes the addend's half in the constant field
beside the RELA entry** (`MVKH fwd-8, A0` -> `007fffe8`; ours `00000068`; edge 437). lnk6x
replaces the field: `linkcheck/addends` links the pairs identically. Fix if wanted: fill the
field too.

**E3. Data in a code section lacks asm6x's `R_C6000_NOCMP` marker relocations** (edge 118,
185, 187, 408, 439, 441). The compilers keep data out of `.text`.

**E4. `.global g` for a symbol neither defined nor referenced is written as an undefined
global; asm6x omits it** (edge 387, 406). lnk6x links it (`linkcheck/global-unused`). `.def d`
undefined: asm6x refuses, ASM6x writes the same undefined global (edge 384).

**E5. asm6x warns and mends where ASM6x refuses**, which is the better behaviour: MVK outside
16 bits (asm6x wraps to -1), NOP 10 (-> 9), `.byte 256`, `.half 65536`, `.align 3` / `0` (-> 1),
`.bss` alignment 3, `LDW *+A4(3)` (offset truncated), `'\n'` (edge 001-004, 015, 126, 211,
244, 257-260, 317, 318).

**E6. Over-acceptances with no shipped consequence.** `||` after a label line, after `[A0]`,
or across a section switch (the p-bit then lands in the new section's data; asm6x refuses;
edge 171, 172, 178, 179); `*+B15` with no offset (137); `LDW *A4, A5:A4` and `LDDW *A4, A5`
(the pair / odd register silently taken as A4 / A5; asm6x refuses; 143, 144); `.usect` without
a label (204); `.byte ext` written as `R_C6000_ABS8` (asm6x: "Invalid data size for
relocation"; 413); `.string "q\"q"` (asm6x: "Cluttered string constant"; 219);
`.sect ".bss"` after `.bss` (asm6x: "Cannot redefine this section"; the `.word` is discarded
here; 216, 325); `MVKL $EXTAB_LP(fwd), A0` (asm6x warns "cannot use built-in function" and
drops the instruction, leaving a 0x1c-byte `.text`; ASM6x encodes MVKL with ABS_L16; 430).

**E7. Two asm6x oddities for the record, not ASM6x's.** `.space -1` makes asm6x write a 512 MB
`.data`; `.sect ".c6xabi.exidx:.text:f"` with no `.text:f`, or an exidx holding a `.byte`,
makes cl6x fail with no message (edge 327, 361, 363).

## Verdict

For what the three compilers write today, ASM6x is a faithful asm6x: the 763 corpus files and
two fresh programs written to reach past them assemble table-identical and link, it is
thread-clean under TSan with 763 files in flight, and no object it produced was rejected by
lnk6x. The faults found sit just outside the compilers' current emissions, but inside the
vocabulary the README claims and the emulator accepts: `.usect` (A1), branch addends (A2),
BNOP's relocation and reach (A3), a negative memory offset (A4), the unit guess behind `||`
(A5), an instruction after data (A6), MPY32 into a pair (A7), `.sect` flags and the NOBITS
data loss (A8), string escapes (A9), and the missing range checks that let a `.bss` alignment
of 0 or a hang out (A10, C1-C3). None needs a redesign; A1-A4, A6, A7, A10-A12 and C1-C3 are
each a few lines, A5 is a unit column in the form tables, A8 and A9 are a rule change each. The
